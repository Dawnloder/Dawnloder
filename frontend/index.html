FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Dawnloader really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no premium tier, no hidden costs. We keep it free by not storing anything and using free hosting tiers.

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, TikTok, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Pinterest, and 1000+ more through yt-dlp.

Can I download private videos?

No. Only publicly accessible content can be downloaded. Private or login-required videos won't work.

Do you store my links or downloads?

Never. Files are processed in a temporary folder and immediately deleted after the download finishes.

Why is it slow sometimes?

Our backend runs on a free tier that sleeps after 15 minutes of inactivity. The first request after sleep takes ~30 seconds to wake up.
